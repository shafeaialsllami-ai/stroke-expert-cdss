5. Drug-Related Problems (DRPs) & Proposed Review Points

The following seven items are transcribed from the case author's proposed DRP assessment; they require verification and should not be interpreted as validated prescribing instructions.

DRP 1 — Aspirin loading dose

Recorded issue: Aspirin 75 mg daily without a documented loading dose.

Proposal recorded by author: 160 mg loading dose, then 75 mg once daily.

Review required: Confirm ischemic stroke diagnosis and brain imaging; exclude intracranial hemorrhage and assess timing, swallowing route, and contraindications before antiplatelet therapy.

DRP 2 — Acyclovir dose documentation

Recorded regimen: 80 mg IV four times daily.

Proposal recorded by author: 10 mg/kg IV every 8 hours (750 mg per dose at 75 kg), with normal renal function.

Review required: Verify indication, weight basis, renal dose adjustment, infusion protocol, hydration, and renal monitoring with the treating team/pharmacist.

DRP 3 — Vancomycin regimen and monitoring

Recorded regimen: 1 g IV twice daily.

Proposal recorded by author: 1.5 g IV every 12 hours with therapeutic drug monitoring and renal-function monitoring.

Review required: Do not adopt a fixed dose without indication-specific target exposure, renal assessment, cultures, body-size assessment, and local vancomycin protocol.

DRP 4 — VTE prophylaxis assessment

Recorded issue: No VTE prophylaxis documented; Padua score recorded as 5.

Proposal recorded by author: Intermittent pneumatic compression now; consider UFH after 3 days if stable and no bleeding.

Review required: Reassess stroke subtype, imaging, mobility, bleeding risk, contraindications, and local VTE guidance. Pharmacologic prophylaxis timing must be clinician-led.

DRP 5 — Dexamethasone regimen/timing

Recorded regimen: 4 mg IV twice daily.

Proposal recorded by author: 10 mg IV every 6 hours for 4 days, timed before/with the first antimicrobial dose.

Review required: Steroid use depends on suspected/confirmed meningitis cause and guideline context; verify regimen and timing against current local/international guidance.

DRP 6 — Phenytoin indication

Recorded issue: Phenytoin 100 mg IV three times daily without a documented seizure.

Proposal recorded by author: Discontinue if there is no seizure indication.

Review required: Check seizure history, EEG/clinical indication, imaging, and whether discontinuation is appropriate.

DRP 7 — Citicoline and vitamin supplementation

Recorded issue: Citicoline and vitamin B complex without a documented indication/deficiency.

Proposal recorded by author: Consider discontinuation if no indication exists.

Review required: Confirm indication, nutritional status, deficiencies, and institutional protocol.

The submitted case describes a 35-year-old male admitted to ICU with left-sided weakness and recorded diagnoses of ICVA and meningitis. The medication chart and laboratory findings are presented as supplied. Seven potential DRPs and two pharmaceutical-care counseling interventions were documented by the case author.
