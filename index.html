Detailed Clinical Treatment Guide & Pharmacotherapy Algorithm

DiPiro 13th Ed & AHA/ASA Guidelines

Critical Safety Alerts – High-Risk Errors

Premature Antiplatelet Use: NEVER give aspirin, clopidogrel, or heparin within 24 hours of thrombolysis (Alteplase/Tenecteplase) unless mechanical thrombectomy requires stenting.
Hemorrhage Rule Out: NEVER administer thrombolytics without a Non-Contrast CT head scan excluding intracranial hemorrhage (ICH).
BP Pre-Thrombolysis: Must lower BP to <185/110 mmHg BEFORE starting Alteplase/Tenecteplase and maintain <180/105 mmHg for 24h post-infusion.
Dosing Error Hazard: Alteplase max dose is strictly 90 mg (0.9 mg/kg). Tenecteplase max stroke dose is 25 mg (0.25 mg/kg bolus). Do NOT use cardiac doses!
Anticoagulant Reversal: Delay in reversing Warfarin/DOAC in active ICH directly increases hematoma expansion and 30-day mortality (46%). Administer reversal immediately.
Symptomatic ICH Recognition: Sudden neuro decline, severe headache, acute nausea/vomiting, or surge in BP during/after IV thrombolytic = STOP infusion immediately & order stat CT.

Acute Ischemic Stroke (AIS) Management Protocol

Covers 87% of all stroke events. Goal: Rapid reperfusion & salvage of the ischemic penumbra.

DiPiro Ch. 42 / AHA Guidelines

Airway & Oxygenation

  • • Maintain $SpO_2 > 94\%$ with supplemental $O_2$ if needed.
  • • Intubate if GCS < 8 or severe bulbar compromise.
  • • Avoid hypoxia; avoid hyperoxia.

Urgent Lab Workup

  • • STAT Blood Glucose: Correct if <60 mg/dL (3.3 mmol/L) or >180 mg/dL.
  • • Platelet count, PT/INR, aPTT (Do not delay thrombolysis for labs unless coagulopathy suspected).
  • • Troponin, Serum Creatinine, Electrolytes.

Brain & Vascular Imaging

  • • Non-Contrast Head CT / MRI: Rule out ICH immediately.
  • • CT Angiography (CTA): Evaluate Large Vessel Occlusion (LVO) in ICA / MCA-M1.
  • • NIHSS score determination prior to intervention.

IV Thrombolytic Therapy (Reperfusion)

Alteplase (rt-PA) Protocol

Class I, LOE A

Window: < 3 hours (Class I, LOE A) or 3 to 4.5 hours from Last Known Well (LKW) (Class I, LOE B-R).

Dose: 0.9 mg/kg total body weight (Maximum 90 mg total).

Administration: 10% as IV Bolus over 1 minute; remaining 90% as IV Infusion over 60 minutes.

Preparation: Reconstitute with sterile water for injection (1 mg/mL). Do not shake.

Diminished benefit/avoid if NIHSS > 25 or >1/3 MCA territory infarct on CT in 3–4.5h window.

Tenecteplase (TNK) Protocol

ESO 2023 / AHA IIb

Window: < 4.5 hours from LKW (especially ideal before Mechanical Thrombectomy).

Dose: 0.25 mg/kg total body weight (Maximum 25 mg single bolus).

Administration: Single rapid IV bolus given over 5 seconds.

Guideline Note: ESO 2023 recommends TNK 0.25 mg/kg as safe/effective alternative to Alteplase. AHA 2019 noted 0.4 mg/kg alternative, but recent data (NOR-TEST 2) showed safety concerns with 0.4 mg/kg dose in high NIHSS. Stick to 0.25 mg/kg.

Thrombolysis Eligibility Criteria (Table 42-4 Summary)

Inclusion Criteria (Must Meet All)

  • Age ≥ 18 years.
  • Clinical diagnosis of ischemic stroke causing measurable neurological deficit.
  • Onset of symptoms / Last Known Well established < 4.5 hours prior to treatment.
  • Blood Pressure safely controlled to < 185/110 mmHg before infusion.

Absolute Contraindications (Exclude if Any Present)

  • Head CT showing acute ICH, SAH, or structural lesion.
  • History of ICH or ischemic stroke within prior 3 months.
  • Active internal bleeding or acute coagulopathy (Plt < 100,000/$mm^3$, INR > 1.7, aPTT > 40s, PT > 15s).
  • Direct Thrombin Inhibitors or DOAC use within prior 48 hours.
  • Treatment-dose LMWH received in prior 24 hours.
  • Severe head trauma in prior 3 months; GI malignancy or GI bleed in prior 21 days.
  • Infective endocarditis, aortic arch dissection, intra-axial intracranial neoplasm.

Mechanical Thrombectomy (EVT) Indications

  • • Strong Recommendation (<6 hours): Anterior circulation Large Vessel Occlusion (ICA or MCA segment M1) within 6h of onset (Class I, LOE A).
  • • Extended Window (6–24 hours): Select patients with ICA/MCA-M1 occlusions with salvageable penumbra tissue on perfusion imaging (DAWN / DEFUSE-3 criteria).
  • • Thrombolysis Bridge: EVT should be performed in addition to IV thrombolysis if eligible; do not delay EVT to observe response to thrombolysis.

Managing Thrombolysis Complications

1. Symptomatic Intracranial Hemorrhage (sICH):

STOP tPA/TNK immediately. STAT Non-Contrast Head CT. Labs: fibrinogen, PT/INR, platelets. Administer Cryoprecipitate 10 units IV (target fibrinogen >150 mg/dL) or Tranexamic Acid (TXA) 1000 mg IV over 10 min.

2. Orolingual Angioedema (1-5% risk, higher with ACEi):

STOP tPA/TNK infusion. Maintain airway. Administer Methylprednisolone 125 mg IV + Diphenhydramine 50 mg IV + Famotidine 20 mg IV. If expanding: Epinephrine 0.3 mg IM / nebulized.

Hemorrhagic Stroke Management Protocol

Includes Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH) & Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (SAH). High 30-day mortality (~46%).

AHA ICH 2022 / SAH 2023 Guidelines

A. Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH) Critical Care

Blood Pressure Lowering in ICH

  • • Presentation SBP 150–220 mmHg: Acute lowering to target SBP of 140 mmHg (range 130–150 mmHg) is safe and improves functional outcomes (Class I, LOE A).
  • • Presentation SBP > 220 mmHg: Aggressive IV infusion reduction (Nicardipine, Clevidipine, Labetalol) is reasonable (Class IIb, LOE C).
  • • WARNING: Acute lowering to SBP < 130 mmHg is potentially harmful and MUST BE AVOIDED. Maintain smooth continuous control without sharp spikes/variability.

Intracranial Pressure (ICP) & Edema Management

  • • Elevate head of bed to 30° and maintain head in midline position.
  • • Osmotic Therapy for Elevated ICP/Edema:
    - Hypertonic Saline (3% or 23.4%): 3% NaCl continuous infusion (target serum $Na^+ 145-155$ mEq/L) or 23.4% NaCl bolus 30 mL for acute herniation.
    - Mannitol 20%: $0.5 - 1 \text{ g/kg}$ IV bolus over 20 min q6h (Target serum osmolality < 320 mOsm/kg).
  • • Seizures: Treat clinical seizures. Prophylactic antiseizure medications are NOT recommended (Class III, LOE B).

B. Aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage (SAH) Protocol

1. Aneurysm Securing & BP Control

• Target SBP < 160 mmHg prior to surgical clipping or endovascular coiling to prevent rebleeding.
• Early repair within 24–72h is paramount.

2. Nimodipine Vasospasm Prophylaxis

• Nimodipine 60 mg orally q4h for 21 consecutive days starting within 96 hours of SAH onset.
• If BP low: 30 mg orally q2h.
BLACK BOX: Oral/NG tube administration ONLY. NEVER administer intravenously (causes fatal arrest).

3. Delayed Cerebral Ischemia (DCI)

• Maintain euvolemia (0.9% NaCl continuous).
• Avoid prophylactic hypervolemia.
• If symptomatic DCI occurs: Induced hypertension with IV vasopressors (Norepinephrine/Phenylephrine) to maintain perfusion.

Algorithm 1: Acute Ischemic Stroke Management Pathway

1

Patient Arrival & Triage (< 10 Minutes)

Action: Assess ABCs, check Point-of-Care Blood Glucose immediately, record LKW (Last Known Well) time, obtain baseline NIHSS.

Target: Dextrose if blood glucose < 60 mg/dL. If CT clear and LKW < 4.5h, proceed to Step 2.

2

Urgent Imaging & Exclusion of ICH (< 20 Minutes)

Action: STAT Non-contrast Head CT + CTA (Head and Neck).

Decision Point:
• Hemorrhage seen? → Transfer to Hemorrhagic Stroke Algorithm.
• No hemorrhage & LKW < 4.5 hours? → Evaluate BP and Thrombolytic Eligibility (Step 3).

3

BP Control & Thrombolytic Administration (< 45 Minutes Door-to-Needle)

Action: If BP > 185/110 mmHg, give Labetalol 10-20 mg IV or start Nicardipine/Clevidipine infusion. Once BP < 185/110 mmHg:

Dose: Alteplase 0.9 mg/kg (10% bolus, 90% over 1 hour) OR Tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg single IV bolus.

Next Step: Check CTA for Large Vessel Occlusion (Step 4).

4

LVO Assessment & Endovascular Thrombectomy (EVT)

Eligibility: LKW within 0–6 hours (or 6–24h with perfusion mismatch) AND CTA shows ICA or MCA-M1 occlusion.

Action: Transfer immediately to Angio Suite for Mechanical Thrombectomy. Do not withhold tPA/TNK.

5

Post-Reperfusion Care & Antiplatelet Timing

• Maintain SBP < 180/105 mmHg for 24h. Neuro checks q15m x2h, q30m x6h, q1h x16h.

• Antiplatelet Initiation: Start Aspirin 160–325 mg daily EXACTLY 24 HOURS post-thrombolytic after follow-up CT scan rules out hemorrhagic transformation.

• If non-thrombolyzed minor stroke (NIHSS ≤3) or high-risk TIA: Start DAPT (Aspirin + Clopidogrel) within 24h for 21 days.

Algorithm 2: Spontaneous Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH) Management

Step 1: Rapid Diagnosis Non-Contrast CT Head. Assess baseline ICH score, hematoma volume, intraventricular extension.
Step 2: Immediate Reversal If on Warfarin, DOAC, or Heparin: Administer Kcentra 4PCC, Idarucizumab, or Andexanet Alfa STAT.
Step 3: Blood Pressure Goal If SBP 150-220: Titrate Nicardipine / Clevidipine IV to maintain target SBP 140 mmHg (range 130-150).
Step 4: ICP & Neurosurgery Consult Neurosurgery for cerebellar hemorrhage >3 cm or hydrocephalus (EVD placement). Osmotic therapy if herniating.

Indication: Acute Ischemic Stroke within 4.5 hours of symptom onset.

Mechanism of Action: Recombinant tissue plasminogen activator. Binds to fibrin in a thrombus and converts trapped plasminogen to plasmin, causing local fibrinolysis.

Recommended Dose: 0.9 mg/kg IV (10% bolus over 1 min, remaining 90% over 60 min). Max Dose: 90 mg total.

Monitoring: BP and neuro status q15m during infusion and for 2 hours post; then q30m for 6h; q1h for 16h.

When to Start/Avoid/Stop: Start ASAP <4.5h. STOP immediately if patient develops severe headache, acute hypertension, nausea, vomiting, or neurological decline.

1. Alteplase vs Tenecteplase (TNK)

Feature Alteplase (rt-PA) Tenecteplase (TNK)
Half-life 3–5 minutes 20–24 minutes (Longer)
Fibrin Specificity Standard Higher (Less systemic breakdown)
Administration 10% bolus + 60 min infusion Single 5-sec bolus
Clinical Advantage FDA approved; extensive history Faster transfer to Cath Lab for EVT; ESO 2023 preferred

2. Antiplatelet Regimens for Noncardioembolic Stroke

Aspirin Monotherapy (50–325 mg/d): Standard baseline secondary prevention (Class I, LOE A).

DAPT (Aspirin + Clopidogrel 75mg):
• Minor Stroke (NIHSS ≤ 3) or High-Risk TIA (ABCD2 ≥ 4): Start <24h, continue for 21 days, then single antiplatelet. (POINT / CHANCE trials).
• Severe Intracranial Stenosis (70–99%): DAPT for up to 90 days, then single agent (SAMMPRIS trial).

• Triple Antiplatelet Therapy (Aspirin + Clopidogrel + Dipyridamole) is HARMFUL (TARDIS trial: high bleeding, no benefit).

Supportive Care & Secondary Prevention Protocol

In-hospital supportive management to prevent medical complications and stroke recurrence.

Glucose & Temp Control

  • • Hyperglycemia: Treat serum glucose > 180 mg/dL with IV or subcutaneous insulin. Target 140–180 mg/dL.
  • • Hypoglycemia (< 60 mg/dL): STAT 10–25 g Dextrose (D50W IV) to prevent stroke mimic worsening.
  • • Fever Control: Maintain normothermia ($T < 38^\circ\text{C} / 100.4^\circ\text{F}$). Administer Acetaminophen.

Dysphagia & DVT Prophylaxis

  • • Dysphagia Screen: NPO until formal swallowing screening completed before oral meds/food to prevent aspiration pneumonia.
  • • VTE Prophylaxis: Intermittent Pneumatic Compression (IPC) devices started on admission.
  • • Pharmacologic VTE Prophylaxis (LMWH Enoxaparin 40 mg SC daily) started 24h post-thrombolytic if immobile.

Long-Term Secondary Targets

  • • Blood Pressure Goal: Target < 130/80 mmHg post-acute phase using Thiazides, ACEi, or ARB.
  • • Lipids: High-intensity Statin (Atorvastatin 80mg) to LDL < 70 mg/dL. Add Icosapent Ethyl 2g BID if Triglycerides 135–499 mg/dL on statin.
  • • Lifestyle: Mediterranean diet, sodium reduction <2g/day, smoking cessation, alcohol limit (≤2 drinks/d men, ≤1 women).
